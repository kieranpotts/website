I'm Kieran. I'm a staff-level software developer based near Bristol and Bath in the south-west of England. I work as an independent consultant and freelancer across three disciplines — software architecture, computer programming, and technical writing — to design, build, and document cloud-based enterprise application software.

My main interests are resilient service-oriented architectures, domain-driven design, and continuous delivery.

This website is a perpetual work in progress, aggregated from AsciiDoc sources I maintain across several repositories. My xref:thoughts::index.adoc[blog] is where I write about the past, present, and future of software development. My xref:standards::index.adoc[technical standards] are the guidelines I've developed for building great software. My xref:garden::index.adoc[digital garden] is a personal wiki of notes, research, and reference material on software design and wider computer science topics. And my xref:bookmarks::index.adoc[bookmarks] are a structured index of everything I've found online that's worth holding on to. There's even more on https://github.com/kieranpotts[GitHub].
